---
name: Closing checklist
description: The steps to close the shop at the end of the day, in order
owner: counter
reviewed: 2026-10-20
---

Start one hour before closing, not at closing time.

## One hour before

- Mark the remaining bread at half price (end-of-day rule).
- Count tomorrow's standing orders on the kitchen board and tell the
  kitchen if anything is unusual.

## At closing

- Lock the door, turn the sign.
- Bag what is left: bread for the food bank crate, pastries to the bin.
- Wipe the counter and the display, empty the crumb trays.

## The till

- Print the terminal report. Reconcile card totals against it and cash
  against the float.
- Note any difference in the day book with the date and your name.
- Leave the float in the till, the rest in the safe.

## Last

- Lights, ovens off (the kitchen does this, but check), alarm on.
