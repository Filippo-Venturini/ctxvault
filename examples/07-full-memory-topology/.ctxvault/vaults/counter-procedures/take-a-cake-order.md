A cake order needs two days' notice. Anything sooner goes to the kitchen as a
question, not as an order.

## Ask, in this order

1. The day and the time of pickup. Check it against the two-day rule and the
   opening hours of that day.
2. Size: how many people it is for. Offer the three sizes on the order sheet,
   do not invent a fourth.
3. The cake: one from the order sheet, or a variation of one. A variation
   is written down word for word.
4. Allergies. Ask even if nobody mentions them. Write the answer down even
   when it is "none".
5. A name and a phone number.

## Then

- Quote the price from the order sheet. A variation is quoted by the kitchen,
  so say "the kitchen will confirm the price by tomorrow".
- Take the deposit written on the order sheet.
- Fill the pickup slip in two copies: one for the customer, one on the
  kitchen board.

## Do not

- Promise a decoration or a wording you have not written down.
- Take an order by phone without a name and a number.
