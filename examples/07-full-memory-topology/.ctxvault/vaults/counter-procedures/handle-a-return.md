---
name: Handle a return
description: What to do when a customer brings something back or says something was wrong
owner: counter
reviewed: 2026-06-02
---

Bread and pastry are not returned for a refund by default. What we do
instead depends on what went wrong.

## Something was wrong with the product

Underbaked, burnt, a foreign object, a wrong order: replace it on the spot
or refund it, the customer chooses. Keep the product, note the tray time
from the label and tell the kitchen the same day.

## The customer changed their mind

Offer a credit note for next time, not a refund. Be kind about it; most
people are fine with a credit note.

## A cake order collected and then complained about

Take the complaint down in writing with the pickup slip number and pass it
to the owner. Do not refund a cake at the counter.

## Always

- Apologise first, regardless of the cause.
- Write it in the day book: what, when, what was done.
