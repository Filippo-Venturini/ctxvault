# Sourdough loaf

The house loaf: a 900 g country sourdough with a dark crust and an open,
slightly sour crumb. Baked every morning, on the shelf from 7:00, usually
sold out by early afternoon.

## Ingredients

Stone-ground wheat flour, water, salt, sourdough starter. Nothing else: no
yeast, no sugar, no fat.

## Allergens

Contains gluten (wheat). Made in a kitchen that handles milk, eggs, nuts,
sesame and soy, so traces are possible.

## How to store it

Keep it in a paper bag or a bread box at room temperature. It stays good for
three days; after that it toasts well for another two. Do not refrigerate,
the crumb dries out. It freezes well when sliced.

## Sizes and prices

Whole loaf (900 g) and half loaf (450 g). Prices are on the shelf tag and in
the price list; the half loaf is cut on request, not pre-cut.

## Common questions

"Is it vegan?" Yes: flour, water, salt and starter only.
"Is it gluten-free?" No. Nothing in the shop is gluten-free.
"Can I order a whole loaf for a specific day?" Yes, with one day's notice at
the counter.
