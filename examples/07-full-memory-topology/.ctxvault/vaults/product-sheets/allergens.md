# Allergens

What is in the shop's products, for anyone who asks at the counter. When in
doubt, say that the kitchen handles all of the following and that traces are
always possible: gluten, milk, egg, nuts, sesame, soy.

## What we do not make

Nothing in the shop is gluten-free, and nothing is guaranteed nut-free. The
sourdough and the focaccia are vegan.

## If a customer has a reaction

Call the emergency number first. Then tell the owner the same day, and note
which product and which batch (the batch time is on the tray label).
