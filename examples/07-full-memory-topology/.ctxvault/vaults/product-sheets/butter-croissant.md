# Butter croissant

A classic croissant laminated with butter over two days. Flaky, with a
honeycomb interior. Baked in two batches, at 7:00 and at 10:00, so the second
batch is still warm for the mid-morning crowd.

## Ingredients

Wheat flour, butter, milk, water, sugar, yeast, salt, egg (for the wash).

## Allergens

Contains gluten (wheat), milk and egg. Made in a kitchen that handles nuts,
sesame and soy.

## Variants

- Plain butter croissant.
- Almond croissant: a day-old croissant filled with almond cream and baked
  again. Contains nuts.
- Chocolate croissant (pain au chocolat): two batons of dark chocolate.
  Contains soy.

## How to store it

Best the same day. Keeps until the next morning in a paper bag; refresh it
for three minutes in a hot oven. Do not microwave.

## Common questions

"Do you have a vegan croissant?" No. The lamination is butter.
"Can I order a box for a meeting?" Yes, boxes of six or twelve with one day's
notice; see the counter for how orders are taken.
