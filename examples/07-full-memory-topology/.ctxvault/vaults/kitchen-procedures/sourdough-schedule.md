The loaf sold on day three starts on day one. Keep the rhythm and the bread
keeps its character.

## Day one, evening

- Feed the starter: equal weights of flour and water. It should double by
  morning; if it does not, the kitchen is too cold, move it near the oven.

## Day two, morning

- Mix: flour, water, salt, starter. Rest thirty minutes before adding salt.
- Four folds over two hours, then bulk until the dough has grown by half.
- Shape, into baskets, into the retarder overnight.

## Day three, 5:30

- Score and bake on the top decks with steam for the first fifteen minutes.
- On the shelf from 7:00.

## Flour

The flour comes from one mill, and the dough is set for that flour. When the
mill changes, expect to adjust the water for a week and say so to the
counter, so they can answer customers who notice.
