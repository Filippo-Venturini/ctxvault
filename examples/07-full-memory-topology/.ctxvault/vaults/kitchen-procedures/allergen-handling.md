The shop cannot promise anything is allergen-free, but it can keep the risk
low and the labels true.

## Order of work

Plain products first, then milk and egg, then nuts, sesame and soy last.
The almond cream and the chocolate batons are the last things opened.

## Tools and trays

- The red tools and the red trays are for nuts, sesame and soy only.
- Trays are labelled with the product and the batch time before they go to
  the shop. The counter reads the label, not the tray.

## Cleaning

Wipe down and change gloves between the plain and the allergen runs. Wash
the red tools separately.

## If something goes wrong

A product that touched the wrong tray is not sold. Tell the counter which
batch time it was, so the sheet at the counter matches.
