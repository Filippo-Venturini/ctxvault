The first bake goes in at 5:30. Both ovens take time to reach temperature,
so this is the first thing done on arrival.

## Deck oven

1. Switch on all three decks. Set the top two for bread, the bottom one for
   focaccia.
2. Fill the steam reservoir. Check the gauge, not the sound.
3. Wait for the temperature light on each deck; it takes about forty
   minutes.

## Convection oven

1. Switch on, set for pastry.
2. Check the fan runs and the door seal is dry.
3. Ready in about twenty minutes.

## While they heat

Shape the first loaves, take the croissants out of the retarder to prove.

## If an oven does not reach temperature

Do not open the door repeatedly. Note the time and the deck, tell the owner,
and move the bake to the other oven. The deck oven is under warranty: do not
touch the elements.
