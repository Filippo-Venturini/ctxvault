# Invoice handling

What comes in, what goes out, and when.

## Supplier invoices

Flour is invoiced monthly by the mill, dairy weekly, eggs on delivery. Every
invoice is matched against the delivery note on the day it arrives; a
mismatch is raised with the supplier within three days or it is paid as
invoiced. Invoices are paid at thirty days, except eggs, paid on delivery.

## Customer invoices

Restaurants and offices with a standing order are invoiced on the first of
the month for the previous month, with the ten percent standing-order
discount applied per line. Cake orders above a set amount are invoiced on
collection if the customer asks; otherwise they go through the till.

## Till reconciliation

The till is reconciled every evening as part of closing: card totals against
the terminal report, cash against the float. A difference above a small
threshold is noted with the date and the name of whoever closed.

## What to keep

Delivery notes for a year, invoices for ten. Everything is scanned the week
it arrives.
