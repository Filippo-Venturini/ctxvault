# Pricing rules

How prices are set and when they change. Internal: the counter quotes the
price list, it does not negotiate.

## Margins

Bread is priced at three times the cost of ingredients; pastries at four
times, because the butter and the two days of lamination are the cost. The
cake counter is priced per order from the ingredient sheet plus a flat
labour amount.

## When prices change

Prices are reviewed twice a year, after the flour contract renews and after
the dairy contract renews. A change is announced on the shelf tags one week
before it applies. Mid-year changes only follow a supplier change.

## Discounts

End-of-day bread is sold at half price in the last hour, never earlier.
Standing orders from restaurants get ten percent off the list price and are
invoiced monthly. No other discounts: a loyalty programme ran for a while and
was closed.

## Card fees

Card payments cost a small fee per transaction. The shop absorbs it; there is
no minimum amount for paying by card any more.
